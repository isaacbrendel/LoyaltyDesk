## Design Context

### Users
Loyalty program analysts and AI engineers reviewing an internal agent workbench. They ask program questions under time pressure, need evidence (SQL/citations), and must approve high-risk mutations. Context: desktop-first, occasional mobile review.

### Brand Personality
Precise, calm, authoritative. Three words: **exacting · lucid · steadfast**. Emotion: confidence that the answer is grounded — never hype.

### Aesthetic Direction
Industrial-editorial instrument panel for loyalty intelligence. Deep teal ink atmosphere with warm amber signal accents (not purple neon, not cream-terracotta cliché). Light work surfaces on a dark field. Display: Fraunces. Body: DM Sans. Anti-references: generic AI dashboards, glassmorphism stacks, purple gradients, emoji-laden empty states, equal card grids.

### Design Principles
1. **Evidence first** — every answer surfaces SQL/semantic proof and agent lineage before flourish.
2. **One job per region** — compose, not dashboard-clutter; progressive disclosure for traces/evals.
3. **State is visible** — routing, reflection, HITL pending must be readable at a glance.
4. **Motion with meaning** — stagger load and status transitions only; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
5. **Harden for real text** — long SQL, redacted PII, error and empty states are first-class.
